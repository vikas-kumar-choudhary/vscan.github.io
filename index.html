Vikas Kumar Choudhary, born in the village of Anandpur urf Bandpur, Baghpat, Uttar Pradesh, is a prominent political strategist, governance thinker, motivational speaker, and the Founder & CEO of V SCAN Alliance (Vikas Strategic Citizens Accountability Network) — a next-generation political consulting and policy firm committed to accountable governance and citizen-first leadership across India.

With over 7 years of extensive experience, Vikas has guided 100+ political leaders and collaborated with 4+ political parties, successfully conceptualizing and leading 100+ political campaigns across 10+ states.

A landmark chapter in Vikas's career was his role as Media & Senior PR Strategist with the State Election Commission, Delhi.

Academically trained in Journalism and Public Administration from the University of Delhi (North Campus).
