Vikas Kumar Choudhary, born in the village of Anandpur urf Bandpur, Baghpat, Uttar Pradesh, is a prominent political strategist, governance thinker, motivational speaker, and the Founder & CEO of V SCAN Alliance (Vikas Strategic Citizens Accountability Network) — a next-generation political consulting and policy firm committed to accountable governance and citizen-first leadership across India.

With over 7 years of extensive experience, Vikas has guided 100+ political leaders and collaborated with 4+ political parties, successfully conceptualizing and leading 100+ political campaigns across 10+ states. His ethical, data-driven approach and deep grassroots understanding have established him as one of India's most trusted voices in political consulting. Prior to founding V SCAN Alliance, he already worked with India's top 1–2 political consulting firms as a Senior Political Consultant.

A landmark chapter in Vikas's career was his role as Media & Senior PR Strategist with the State Election Commission, Delhi — where he led multidisciplinary teams of journalists and PR professionals, created large-scale voter awareness campaigns, and was directly responsible for increasing voter polling percentages across the capital. He managed PR operations and coordinated with all key stakeholders at the highest levels of governance.

Academically trained in Journalism and Public Administration from the University of Delhi (North Campus), Vikas cleared competitive entrances for institutions including Jamia Millia Islamia and Makhanlal Chaturvedi National University. He earned merit-based scholarships, participated in student rights movements, and represented India in national and overseas MUN conferences on global issues. He also held senior consulting roles at leading MNCs Henry Harvin and Data Trend, and has been selected for 5+ overseas conferences on governance, health, and poverty — delivering 20+ motivational talks inspiring the next generation of leaders.

V SCAN Alliance is a next-generation political consulting and policy firm founded on the belief that democracy deserves better strategy, and citizens deserve better governance.

Unlike traditional consulting firms, V SCAN extends its role beyond elections — integrating both pre-election strategy and post-election governance to ensure policy implementation is grounded in real public needs rather than top-down assumptions.
